# Splat skin weight cleanup

Open **Rigging → Skin Weights** and choose a bound splat shape. The component indices refer to the original asset throughout a pose change. **Hold selected influence** fixes that joint’s weight on every original, including existing zeros. The hold belongs to the binding and survives save, reopen and undo.

**Smooth selected weights** uses the current range or viewport selection. Set the distance in object units and a strength from zero to one. Preview the selected joint’s resulting weights on the posed centers, then apply or cancel. **Rigging → Paint Skin Weights → Smooth** uses the same calculation with the brush’s strongest falloff per original during a stroke. Release commits once. Escape discards the stroke. Alt temporarily orbits.

The neighborhood consists of up to eight other active original centers within the distance in the object’s original bind space. Equal distances prefer the lower original index. Each neighbor contributes equally. Each row blends from its original weights toward the average. Held weights consume capacity first. The strongest remaining unlocked influences share the residual mass proportionally in the remaining four slots. No neighbors leaves a row unchanged. Invalid rows or impossible allocation reject the entire operation.

**Mirror skin weights** offers the YZ, XZ and XY planes through the asset origin. Select the source side. Pair corresponding influences using the two joint menus. An unpaired joint keeps its ID. The nearest original on the source side, within the matching distance of the reflected target, supplies the row. Center-plane and source-side rows stay untouched. The preview reports matches, unmatched targets and changed rows. Hold rules apply at the target. Object transforms and posed joints do not alter the rest-space correspondence.

## Scene-visible painting and selection

Choose **Scene visible** in Splat Components, Skin Weights viewport selection, Paint Skin Weights, Paint Splat Masks or Paint Splat Attributes. Through and Front centers are available for other workflows. The component picker, lasso, brush selection and painting project the evaluated center of each original index. Generated elastic fill can occlude a center, but never receives an original ID.

Scene visible captures the authored camera-depth pass once per gesture. Opaque depth-writing polygons and alpha-tested cutouts contribute. A Gaussian contributes where its individual effective fragment alpha reaches 0.5. Blended polygon materials, gizmos and preview markers are excluded. The center must be on screen and its positive camera-axis distance must match the nearest sampled depth within the greater of 0.0001 scene units and 0.05% distance. Sampling uses the pixel containing the center. Background pixels fail. Overlapping transparent splats do not receive an accumulated-alpha visibility score and silhouettes are quantized to the depth image. Front centers uses nearest projected centers per four-CSS-pixel cell on the edited object.

The depth image’s longest edge is capped at 2,048 pixels, with at most 16 MiB of packed pixel data for a square view. GPU readback uses an asynchronous WebGL2 fence. A lost context, readback failure or eight-second timeout aborts the operation. Scene, pose, camera or viewport changes invalidate the captured frame and Escape discards pending work. The existing Through and Front centers modes remain available. Original centers are projected in chunks of 4,096. Skin paint accepts at most 500,000 projected centers, 20,000 edited originals per operation and 200,000 stored overrides per shape.

The cleanup neighborhood index supports 200,000 source originals and yields every 2,048 rows. Region preview yields every 128 targets. Candidate comparisons stop at 16,384 per local search or eight million per operation. Reduce the radius or region if a limit is reached. At most 5,000 cleanup preview markers are drawn.

## Original example and checks

Open `examples/skin-cleanup-study.msb`. The front wrist, rear torso, mesh occluder and separate splat occluder overlap in the initial camera. A viewport-wide Scene visible box on **Wrist and torso** selects IDs 0, 4, 5 and 6. Through selects all seven. Frame 24 shifts the wrist through its left-joint weight. Choose the spine influence and hold it, then pair left and right and mirror positive to negative across YZ with distance 0.25. Three targets match and one remains unmatched. The spine retains its exact 0.2 weight. Regenerate the bundled scene with `node scripts/create-skin-cleanup-study.mjs`.

The workflow follows Maya’s influence hold, smooth brush and explicit mirror direction. Musubi uses object-local rest centers for neighborhood association because Gaussian assets have no mesh adjacency. The UI and implementation are original and use the existing Three.js 0.180.0 and Spark 2.4.0 installation.
